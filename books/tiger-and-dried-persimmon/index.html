Korean Folktales for Little Readers · Book 1

The Tiger and the Dried Persimmon

A Korean Folktale · Retold by Bright Lee

The biggest tiger in Korea is afraid of... a dried persimmon?

Paperback on Amazon Kindle on Amazon

On a cold, snowy night, a hungry tiger creeps up to a little straw-roofed house. Inside, a baby is crying. “Hush, or the fox will come!” The baby cries on. “Hush, or the bear will come!” Still crying. “Look, a TIGER!” Even louder!

Then the mother says one mysterious word, gotgam, and the crying stops. What terrible creature could be scarier than a tiger? The tiger runs to hide in the dark cowshed, just as a cow thief comes creeping in...

Funny, a little spooky, and full of lines to say together.
